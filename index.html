What we do

Everything your business needs from an accountant

From your first day trading to planning your retirement, one team handles it, so you only explain things once.

Accounts & compliance

Your year-end paperwork done properly and on time, so Companies House and HMRC stay happy.

  • Annual accounts and statutory audits
  • Company secretarial and statutory returns
  • Grant claims

Tax

We work to keep your tax as low as the law allows, and explain every figure.

  • Self Assessment and corporation tax
  • VAT returns, schemes and planning
  • Estate, trust and inheritance tax planning
  • Help with HMRC enquiries

Bookkeeping & payroll

Hand over the admin, or let us set you up to do it yourself with confidence.

  • Bookkeeping at your premises or ours
  • Payroll, RTI and pension auto-enrolment
  • Monthly or quarterly management accounts

Starting a business

Get the foundations right from day one.

  • Sole trader, partnership or limited company?
  • Registering with HMRC and Companies House
  • Business plans, cashflow and funding proposals

Growth & advice

Numbers you can actually make decisions with, and someone to talk them through.

  • Business planning and forecasts
  • Systems and accounting software set-up
  • Support dealing with your bank

Pensions & protection

We introduce you to a trusted independent financial adviser and work alongside them.

  • Pensions and retirement planning
  • ISAs and investments
  • Life, critical illness and medical cover
